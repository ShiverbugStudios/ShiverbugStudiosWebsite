Accessibility

We want this site to work for everyone who visits it, and we aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we’ve done

  • Text and controls meet AA contrast, and the main text colours meet AAA.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, focus is always visible, and there’s a link at the top of each page to skip straight to the content.
  • Buttons, links and form fields are at least 44 by 44 pixels.
  • The game footage is silent, has a pause button, and doesn’t play at all if your device is set to reduce motion. Nothing else on the site moves on its own.
  • Photos and artwork have text descriptions. The cut-out characters used as decoration are hidden from screen readers, because they don’t say anything the text doesn’t.
  • Pages are built with proper headings and landmarks, and still work with styles or scripts switched off.
  • Text can be resized to 200 percent, and the layout reflows down to a 320 pixel wide screen without scrolling sideways.

What isn’t right yet

  • The prototype trailer on the press page has no captions or audio description. It shows the game as it was in 2025 and will be replaced.
  • The two short clips in the co-development gallery are silent and have no audio description. The written description beside each covers what they show.
  • The team’s profiles link to portfolio sites we don’t run, and we can’t vouch for how accessible those are.

Tell us

If something on the site doesn’t work for you, or you need anything here in a different format, email contact@shiverbugstudios.com and tell us what you were trying to do. We’ll fix what we can and reply either way.

Last reviewed September 2026. This statement is our own assessment, not an external audit.
