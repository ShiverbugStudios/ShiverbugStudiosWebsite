Get in touch

The form below and contact@shiverbugstudios.com both land in the same inbox, and a person reads everything that arrives there.

Which way to go

  • Work for your studio

    Use the form and pick co-development. Say what you need and roughly when; a paragraph is plenty.

  • Press

    Email is quickest. The fact sheet, screenshots and logos are already in the press kit.

  • Jobs and internships

    Teesside students apply through Student Futures. Everyone else, read this first, then send a link to your work.

  • Playing the game

    Questions about Out of Water are best asked on our Discord, where the rest of the team can answer too.
