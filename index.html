We make games you play side by side.

Shiverbug is an independent studio in Middlesbrough. We’re making Out of Water, a split-screen platformer for two: one turtle, one seagull, one sofa. We also lend our artists, designers and programmers to other studios.

Meet Out of Water Work with us

Our first game

Out of Water

A 3D platformer collectathon for two players on one screen. One of you is Koopa, a turtle. The other is Kiko, a seagull. Neither can finish the journey alone.

KoopaThe heavy one. Rolls, slams, and is the only one who can dive.

KikoThe quick one. Dashes, glides, and gets up where Koopa can’t.

Co-development

Short on hands? Borrow ours.

The people making Out of Water also make art and code for other studios: concept art, stylised and hard-surface 3D, level design and gameplay programming, in Unity or Unreal and in your pipeline.

  • Concept art

    Characters, creatures, props and places, drawn to explain how something plays as well as how it looks.

  • Stylised 3D

    Environments, props and characters with clear shapes and a lot of personality.

  • Hard surface

    Vehicles, weapons and mechanical props, modelled accurately from reference.

  • Design and code

    Level design, game and narrative design, and gameplay programming.

So far

We started in 2025. This is what has happened since, oldest first.

  1. May 2025

    Three Teesside University graduates, Lewis Mennim, Ryan Hughes and Garrett Windus, set up Shiverbug Studios Ltd in Middlesbrough.

  2. June 2025

    Picked as one of 21 teams for Tranzfuser 2025, the government-funded programme for new graduate studios, with Teesside Launchpad as our local hub.

  3. September 2025

    Took our prototype, then called Koopa and Kiko, to ProtoPlay in Dundee, and came home as the Tranzfuser 2025 Public Vote Winner.

  4. November 2025

    Joined Game Republic, the games industry network for the North of England.

  5. January 2026

    Started the game again in a fresh Unity 6 project, the one that became Out of Water, and gave the studio a new logo and a mascot: Shana the Shiverbug, the moth on every page of this site.

  6. February 2026

    Told the world what the game is about. A turtle, a seagull and five stolen gems.

  7. April 2026

    Put Out of Water in front of players at the Odyssey video games and toy festival in Harrogate.

  8. May 2026

    The first of our Teesside interns joined the team.

  9. July 2026

    Seven of us went down to Develop:Brighton.
