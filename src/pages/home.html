We make games you play side by side.

Shiverbug is an independent studio in Middlesbrough. We're making Out of Water, a split-screen platformer for two: one turtle, one seagull, one sofa. We also lend our artists, designers and programmers to other studios.

Our first game

Out of Water

A 3D platformer collectathon for two players on one screen. One of you is Koopa, a turtle. The other is Kiko, a seagull. Neither can finish the journey alone.

{{ img("stickers/koopa", "", sizes="64px", near=160) }}

KoopaThe heavy one. Rolls, slams, and is the only one who can dive.

{{ img("stickers/kiko", "", sizes="64px", near=160) }}

KikoThe quick one. Dashes, glides, and gets up where Koopa can't.
