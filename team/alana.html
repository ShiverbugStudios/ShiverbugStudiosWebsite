Team / Alana

Alana

3D Animator (she/her)

passionate about character animation and stylised worlds.

I’m a 3D Animation student at Teesside University with a background in Environmental Design. I’m especially interested in character animation, stylised visuals and visual storytelling, and I enjoy bringing personality and emotion into characters through movement.

I mainly work with Maya and Blender, and I like exploring different workflows and styles to develop new ideas. My design background has also given me a strong interest in composition, atmosphere and world-building, which I enjoy combining with animation to create expressive characters and engaging worlds.
