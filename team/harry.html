Team / Harry

Harry

3D Character Artist (he/him)

bang bang character art man

I'm a 3D Character Artist who loves making these cute little creatures and the super cool big guys. I sculpt in ZBrush, retopologize (and hard-surface) in Blender and texture in Substance Painter. I'm out there and lovin' every minute of it!

Working with concept artists is my favourite part of being a 3D Artist, course I enjoy makin' my own stuff but the feeling of making a grand idea come to life, seeing it move around in a 3D space is unreal. In my free time I like sculpting my own characters part of a dark fantasy book I'm writing for fun. I get my inspirations from fantasy series' like Game of Thrones and anime like Attack on Titan, then in games it's gotta be the Legend of Zelda series. Making characters has to be one of the most fun things in existence but I could be biased.
