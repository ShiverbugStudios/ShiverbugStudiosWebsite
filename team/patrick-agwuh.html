Team / Patrick Agwuh

On the team

Patrick Agwuh

3D Animator

i draw things

I’m a First-Class 3D Animation graduate from Teesside University with a passion for animation, film, and indie animation. I grew up inspired by Nickelodeon cartoons, Disney and DreamWorks films, and Cartoon Network, which continue to influence the way I approach character, storytelling, and visual style.

I’m always excited to try new things, experiment with different techniques, and learn new software. I particularly enjoy working with Maya, Procreate, and Premiere Pro, and I’m currently learning Blender to expand my 3D skills and workflow.

I’m always creating, whether that’s developing animation projects, experimenting with new ideas, or exploring different styles. I’m also a big fan of games such as Overwatch, Brawl Stars, Rivals of Aether 2, and Disney Infinity, and I enjoy taking inspiration from the character design, animation, worlds, and visual styles found in games.

I’m passionate about storytelling through animation and would love to continue developing my skills while working on creative projects within the animation, film, and games industries.
